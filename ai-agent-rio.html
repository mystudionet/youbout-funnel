What Rio does.

  • Replies to every review on Google, Yelp, TripAdvisor, Facebook and more
  • Writes in your tone, warm, casual, or formal, you decide once
  • Mentions the customer's actual experience: the dish, the service, the visit
  • Works around the clock, so no review sits unanswered overnight

A day with Rio.

A customer posts a five star review at 11pm. By the time you open the shop, Rio has already thanked them by name, mentioned what they loved, and kept your profile looking alive and cared for. When a rough review lands, Rio answers with grace and invites the customer to talk, so readers see a business that listens.

Questions about Rio.

Will Rio's replies sound robotic?

No. Rio writes in the tone you choose and references specifics from each review, the meal, the staff member, the visit. Most customers can't tell the reply wasn't typed by the owner.

Can I approve replies before they're published?

Yes. You can run Rio fully automatic or set it to draft mode, where every reply waits for your one tap approval.

How does Rio handle negative reviews?

Calmly and professionally. Rio acknowledges the experience, avoids arguments, and invites the customer to continue privately, which is exactly what future readers want to see.

Which review platforms does Rio cover?

Google, Yelp, TripAdvisor, and Facebook on the Gold plan, with expanded coverage of 20+ platforms on Premium.
