FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When is the best moment to ask members for a review?

Youbout automates the timing — typically after the first few weeks of membership or after a milestone, when motivation and satisfaction peak.

Can we run it across multiple locations?

Yes — Premium manages up to five locations from one dashboard, each with its own review flows and pages.

How do leaderboards work?

Each team member gets a personal review link or QR; the dashboard shows who generated what, so you can reward the top collector monthly.

What about members who had a bad experience?

They can reach management privately through your branded page before posting publicly — so you fix the issue, not fight it online.
