FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do my guests actually leave a review?

They scan a QR code at the table or receive an automatic text or WhatsApp message after their visit. It opens your branded review page, they tap a star rating, and they're taken straight to Google, Yelp, or TripAdvisor to post.

Can Youbout hide bad reviews?

No, and no legitimate tool can. What Youbout does is let unhappy guests reach you privately first, so you can fix the problem directly, while happy guests are guided to share publicly.

Which review platforms work for restaurants?

Google, Yelp, TripAdvisor, and Facebook on every plan, with OpenTable and expanded coverage on higher plans.

Will the AI replies sound like a robot?

The Reply Agent writes in your tone, warm, casual, or formal, and naturally mentions specifics from each review, like the dish the guest loved. You can review replies before they publish or let them go out automatically.

How fast can we start?

Most restaurants are collecting reviews the same day: connect your profiles, print your QR codes, and the system takes over. Every plan starts with a 30 day free trial, no credit card required.
